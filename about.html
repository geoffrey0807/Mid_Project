Meet Geoff

Hello! I'm Geoffrey Dael, an aspiring content creator and beginner violinist currently studying at the City College of Calamba.

I am passionate about music and storytelling, and I love sharing my experiences with others. Through this website, I aim to showcase my progress and skills by creating videos. I love seeing people get interested in music, and I want to help more people embrace that joy.
