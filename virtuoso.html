1782 - 1840

Niccolo Paganini

▼


The Italian violinist began musical training as a child and became famous for dazzling technique and compositions such as the 24 Caprices. Build technique in service of musical expression, and develop your own forte.

1844 - 1908

Pablo de Sarasate

▼


Born in Pamplona, Spain, he was performing as a child and entered the Paris Conservatoire at 12. He later became known as both a virtuoso and a composer. What you can learn from him is that precision and elegance can make difficult music sound natural.

Fritz Kreisler

▼

The Viennese violinist entered the Vienna Conservatory as a child prodigy and began composing at a young age. He later wrote and arranged pieces that are still part of the violin repertoire.
